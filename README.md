# CREST — Contract-Relative Ecological State Theory

CREST studies the state structure induced when an ecological system is observed at one temporal boundary while relevant distinctions arrive retrospectively from history, remain hidden transversely in the latent present, and are exposed prospectively by legal future queries.

The trajectory-first question remains:

> **Why can a finite ecological state exist at all in a world whose relevant dynamics, interactions, mechanisms, and scientific observables depend on context?**

An ecological state is a **scientifically licensed compression of a temporally extended ecological world**. In the current finite formulation:

> **The present is a zero-duration observational cut; ecological state is the least quotient induced on that cut by the retrospective, transverse, and prospective distinctions that must be preserved.**

At time \(t\), an observation map

\[
O_t:\Omega\to Y_t
\]

induces the visible baseline

\[
B_t=\ker O_t.
\]

The present is this cut, not a finite-width temporal coordinate and not the state itself. For one visible value \(y\),

\[
L_t(y)=O_t^{-1}(y)
\]

is the fiber of raw possibilities hidden behind that observation. The finite theory treats `zero-duration` as an idealized indexed boundary; it does not claim a proved shrinking-window \(\varepsilon\to0\) or continuous-time germ theorem.

## Snapshot sufficiency at the cut

Snapshot sufficiency is a factorization question. For a required state map \(q_t:\Omega\to Q_t\), the visible present is sufficient exactly when

\[
O_t(\omega)=O_t(\omega')
\Longrightarrow
q_t(\omega)=q_t(\omega').
\]

A same-cut pair separated by the adequate state is therefore a finite witness that the visible present is insufficient for the declared contract.

## Primitive responsibilities before state

- **retrospective / MLTR:** root law and raw replacement path first; carried maps second; retained history is a quotient of immutable histories by equality of carried semantics. This is the left-of-cut structure.
- **transverse latent present / MRM:** primitive candidate laws first; response types derived from declared response tables inside a visible-cut fiber. This is candidate-safe latent response structure, not complete ontic mechanism identity.
- **prospective / CCOC:** controlled law plus legal right-of-cut query grammar first; response equivalence derived from legal traces.
- **CED:** downstream evidence licensing, not a fourth temporal-position responsibility.

For compatibility with the earlier CREST vocabulary:

- **future sufficiency** = CCOC prospective/open-composition responsibility;
- **semantic coherence** = MLTR inherited-semantic/history responsibility;
- **mechanism robustness** = MRM latent-response responsibility;
- **evidence licensing** = downstream CED identification/reportability.

The dependency direction is

\[
\text{primitive companion objects}
\longrightarrow
\text{retrospective / transverse / prospective equivalences and access}
\longrightarrow
J_t.
\]

The dependency DAG is regression-tested to be acyclic. The final cut-state does not define the pre-state structures that later constrain it.

## Strict realizability boundary

Three no-go facts prevent overinterpreting arbitrary refinement cascades as literal temporal companions:

1. fixed precomputed responsibility partitions on a one-class baseline cannot generate positive interaction merely by common refinement;
2. a zero-debt post-cut audit preserving immutable MLTR history cannot later be activated by that history partition; and
3. under one fixed MRM grammar, candidate-safe zero debt implies a singleton response type.

The old marked-cycle result \(\Delta=\log_2 n-1\) and old fixed-closure three-way result \(b-\log_2 3\) remain exact **abstract fixed-closure extrema**, not the flagship companion-derived claim.

## v0.7 flagship: cut geometry and sparse prospective access

The current flagship does not assign coalition values directly and does not claim mathematical novelty for Möbius/Harsanyi accounting.

CREST first derives two semantic structures inside the temporal-boundary problem:

- MLTR raw routes are quotiented by equality of their complete carried terminal maps, giving retrospective history modes;
- MRM primitive candidate laws are quotiented by equality of their complete declared response tables, giving transverse latent-present response types.

Prospective refinement is then controlled by two distinct structures:

1. a **minimum prerequisite set** specifying which pre-state structures must be retained before a prospective query is well formed; and
2. a **semantic access relation** specifying on which combinations of those retained semantic structures the query is actually addressable.

Let

\[
N=|H_{\min}|\,|\Theta|
\]

be the number of retrospective × transverse semantic pairs and let

\[
k=|A_f|
\]

be the number of pairs on which an \(m\)-bit prospective decoder is licensed. When both H and Θ are syntactic prerequisites, the exact grand-coalition quotient is

\[
\boxed{|Q_{H\Theta F}|=(N-k)+k2^m.}
\]

The three-way state dividend is

\[
\boxed{
d_{H\Theta F}
=
\log_2\frac{(N-k)+k2^m}{N}.}
\]

For fixed \(N\) and \(k>0\),

\[
\boxed{
d_{H\Theta F}=m-\log_2(N/k)+o(1).}
\]

Thus **prerequisite structure fixes the interaction order, whereas prospective semantic coverage controls its magnitude**. Complete addressability is only the boundary case \(k=N\).

## Shallow-lake worked interpretation

The shallow-lake case is a worked ecological interpretation with an executable finite counterfactual model. Four lake possibilities share one coarse visible status and cross two retrospective modes with two transverse latent-response types. For each declared restoration target, the code asks whether the target factors through no interface, H only, Θ only, or both, and then substitutes one interface counterfactually while holding the other fixed.

The executable minimum prerequisite sets are

\[
R_{\rm status}=\varnothing,
\quad
R_{\rm legacy}=\{H\},
\quad
R_{\rm mechanism}=\{\Theta\},
\quad
R_{\rm composed}=\{H,\Theta\}.
\]

The composed target has only two outputs yet cannot factor through H alone or Θ alone. This is a **literature-grounded finite interpretation, not empirical validation**, and its parity-style target is a minimal formal witness of joint dependence rather than a biological law.

## Scope firewall

The current result is finite, exact, and conditional on declared companion semantic outputs and legal prospective access structure. CREST does **not** claim:

- that retrospective, transverse latent response, and prospective structure exhaust every ecological state responsibility;
- a unique natural decomposition of every ecological system into these roles;
- that every prospective decoder requires both companion interfaces;
- that complete semantic access is automatic;
- that prerequisite sets or access relations can be inferred from state accounting alone;
- that the shallow-lake finite interpretation is empirical validation;
- statistical confounding;
- a proved continuous-time shrinking-window or infinitesimal-germ theorem;
- stochastic, infinite-state, delayed-observation, or approximate generality.

The present is a zero-duration **observational cut in the finite representation**, not a metaphysical theorem about physical instants.
