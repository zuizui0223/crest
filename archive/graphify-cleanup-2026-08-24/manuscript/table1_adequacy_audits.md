This table is manuscript-facing. The four rows are **inputs to one joint state construction**, not four rival definitions of ecological state. CREST does not claim that the four audits are exhaustive, mutually independent, or historically first.

## Joint-state synthesis

Once the four obligations are represented as refinement closures on one **declared admissible finite common carrier** \(U\), CREST-J1 defines

\[
\boxed{
J=(C_\Gamma\vee C_\mathcal H\vee C_\Theta\vee C_{D,T})(B)
}
\]

as the **unique coarsest / least-information partition satisfying all four obligations**. The CREST state of world/configuration \(u\) is \([u]_J\).

This conditional joint minimality does not imply a globally unique state across different carriers or contracts. J3/J6 separately test whether a suitable common carrier exists. Even when \(J\) exists, a deterministic full-state report is licensed only when the evidence partition resolves it, \(J\preceq E_D\).

## Caption

**Table 1. Four scientific obligations that constrain one contract-relative ecological state.** Future sufficiency, semantic coherence, mechanism robustness, and evidential/target resolution can invalidate a proposed state merge for different reasons and therefore require different local remedies. They are not presented as four competing state definitions. Conditional on one admissible finite common carrier, CREST-J1 combines the four obligations into the unique coarsest joint partition \(J\); the state of a configuration is its block \([u]_J\). This is contract-relative joint minimality, not an intrinsic or cross-contract partition of nature, and evidence may still be too coarse to identify the required block.
