# Biology & Philosophy fit check — 2026-08-19

## Decision

Keep **Biology & Philosophy** as the primary target for the CREST philosophy paper.

## Why the current manuscript fits

The paper's center is philosophical rather than a technical theorem catalogue: it asks what scientific commitment is made when heterogeneous ecological configurations are treated as the same state. The formal companion results are used as disciplined counterexamples, repair criteria, and reporting boundaries for that philosophical question.

Recent Biology & Philosophy articles continue to publish work on conceptual plurality/unity in biological concepts, plural epistemic aims and adequate-for-purpose modelling, and the conceptual structure of mechanisms/pathways. Those neighboring topics support the journal-fit judgment for a paper about contract-relative ecological state representation.

Examples checked on 2026-08-19:

- *How to balance conceptual unity and plurality: the case of the individualized niche concept* (Biology & Philosophy, 2025), which analyzes unity and plurality in a biological concept.
- *The pursuitworthiness of informational biology* (Biology & Philosophy, 2025), which explicitly discusses plural epistemic aims and cites adequate-for-purpose modelling.
- *On mechanisms, pathways and their models* (Biology & Philosophy, 2025), which treats conceptual/model questions about causal structure.

## Manuscript positioning to preserve

1. Lead with the philosophical question about ecological state equivalence, not with CREST as a software/tool framework.
2. Treat CCOC, MLTR, MRM, and CED as theorem-grounded anchors for four distinct obligations, not four mini-papers.
3. Keep the anti-grandiosity boundary explicit: no exhaustiveness, independence, commutation, joint minimality, or historical-firstness claim for the four-audit taxonomy.
4. Keep the practical philosophical payoff explicit: the failure type determines whether the proper response is more state information, semantic repair, ambiguity-preserving prediction/discrimination, or evidential restraint.
5. Do not pad toward an arbitrary word-count target. Add material only if it sharpens an objection, distinction, or ecological/philosophical implication.

## Final fit risk

The main desk-review risk is not lack of formal sophistication; it is the opposite: allowing theorem-family notation or internal program labels to dominate the philosophical argument. Final editing should therefore keep the pollination example and state-equivalence question visible while minimizing repository/program-management language.
