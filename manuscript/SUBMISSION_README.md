## Claim boundary

Do not claim that the four audits are exhaustive, mutually independent, historically first, or that there is one joint state independent of scientific contract.

Do claim, with its premises, the existing conditional result:

> on one declared admissible finite common carrier, J1 yields the unique coarsest state partition satisfying the four declared representational obligations.

Pairwise audit commutation is not required. A one-pass combination of separately minimized audit outputs can fail, while fair repeated refinement reaches the unique least common fixed point.

The companion roles remain distinct inputs to the joint state:

- CCOC: open-future sufficiency and exact interface inflation;
- MLTR: inherited semantic coherence under structural replacement;
- MRM: prediction that is safe across retained response mechanisms;
- CED: target-safe required resolution and evidence-relative licensing of reports.

CREST synthesis owns the cross-contract carrier/state/repair/comparison results J1–J7 and O1; it does not absorb the companion headline theorems.
