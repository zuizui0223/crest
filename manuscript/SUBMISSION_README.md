Do not claim that the four audits are exhaustive, mutually independent, commuting, jointly minimal, or historically first as a general theory of representational adequacy. Do not turn the companion theorem families into four miniature papers inside CREST. CREST owns the higher-level philosophical question: **what scientific commitment is made when ecological configurations are treated as the same state?**

The companion roles remain distinct:

- CCOC: open-future sufficiency and exact interface inflation;
- MLTR: inherited semantic coherence under structural replacement;

- CED: evidence-relative licensing of target reports.
