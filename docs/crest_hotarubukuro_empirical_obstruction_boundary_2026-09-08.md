# Hotarubukuro empirical obstruction quantification boundary

## Decision

The archived Hotarubukuro CREST replay provides real numeric evidence diagnostics, but it does **not** yet identify exact CCOC, MLTR, MRM, or joint CREST state-information bits.

This distinction is essential. The current obstruction-spectrum machinery computes exact finite state debt only after all audits are declared on one common finite carrier. The locked Hotarubukuro replay instead supplies statistical evidence about several target questions without supplying the finite structural contracts needed to convert those results into exact obstruction partitions.

The machine-readable boundary is tracked in:

- `artifacts/hotarubukuro_empirical_obstruction_quantification_2026-09-08.json`.

## What is numerically recovered from data

The replay uses a binary present snapshot, `white` versus `pigmented`, over 1,305 spatial cells: 674 pigmented and 631 white.

### Broad environmental differentiation

The locked result reports:

- observed state excess: **0.052133**;
- state posterior-predictive p: **0.00998**;
- conditional-intensity posterior-predictive p: **0.26347**.

The licensed reading is target-specific: binary pigmentation state is informative for the broad differentiation question. This does not imply that the same binary state is sufficient for every other scientific contract.

### Local Bombus mechanism question

Across 67 non-overlapping 5-km sharp-transition pairs:

- mean pigmented-minus-white Bombus support: **0.03590**;
- median difference: **-0.00277**;
- proportion positive: **0.493**;
- one-sided p: **0.02716**;
- BH q: **0.08148**;
- raw-support sensitivity p: **0.26715**.

These results do not license a deterministic pollinator-mediated selection mechanism. They therefore establish an evidential boundary, not an exact MRM mechanism-debt value.

### Human-context question within pigmented cells

The replay reports:

- relative-isolation rho among pigmented cells: **0.285498**;
- relative-isolation rho among white cells: **0.078506**;
- pigmented relative-natural-map upper-tail p: **0.000900**;
- count-conditioned upper-tail p: **0.003996**;
- direct relative-colour-contrast upper-tail p: **0.158584**.

The licensed reading is that the binary pigmentation snapshot is insufficient for this target because a reproducible continuous human-context overlay remains within the pigmented block. The evidence does **not** identify a discrete provenance class. Therefore CREST must not invent one merely to obtain a convenient finite bit count.

## Why exact obstruction bits are not yet identified

### CCOC

CCOC bits require a declared finite carrier, a baseline partition, closed and opened legal future grammars, and an exact response-equivalence contract. The empirical replay does not contain those objects. Therefore a CCOC bit value cannot be inferred from the environmental or pollinator p-values.

### MLTR

MLTR history or transport debt requires a source system, a target system, a declared replacement relation, accepted source labels, and—when history debt is requested—a finite family of replacement routes. Those are not identified by the replay. The human-context gradient is not itself an MLTR route map.

### MRM

MRM bits require a finite candidate mechanism or response-type family and declared intervention-response signatures. The Bombus analysis shows that the current evidence does not resolve the causal mechanism, but a p-value does not determine how many exact response types remain. Therefore the MRM state debt remains unquantified.

### Joint CREST

Joint debt, Shapley attribution, and Möbius interaction terms require the three structural audits to act on the same finite carrier. Because the empirical replay does not yet provide the individual finite audits, a joint obstruction spectrum is not identified either.

## What is already operationally useful

The empirical replay nevertheless gives a concrete bridge from evidence to the next contract-building step.

For the Bombus branch, the immediate discriminating channels are:

1. standardized direct visitation;
2. single-visit conspecific pollen deposition.

For the human-provenance branch, the immediate channels are:

1. dated occurrence and historical provenance;
2. reference-aware population genetic assignment.

These measurements are not chosen because they increase sample size in the abstract. They are chosen because they break specific distinctions that the current aggregate proxies leave unresolved.

## Quantification rule going forward

The empirical workflow should therefore be:

```text
locked empirical result
        ↓
identify which target-specific distinction remains unresolved
        ↓
declare a finite application contract only where scientifically justified
        ↓
construct CCOC / MLTR / MRM audits on one common carrier
        ↓
compute exact obstruction spectrum in bits
        ↓
compare the required partition with the available evidence
```

The key prohibition is the reverse move: do not discretize a continuous empirical gradient or turn a significance statistic into a finite obstruction cardinality merely to obtain a number.

## Current conclusion

Hotarubukuro now supplies **real empirical numbers for state adequacy and evidence insufficiency**, while the exact obstruction-bit layer remains deliberately unresolved. This is not a failure of the theory; it identifies the missing bridge objects precisely. The next empirical CREST result should be the first application in which those bridge objects are independently declared and defended, after which the existing obstruction-spectrum code can return an exact CCOC/MLTR/MRM joint profile without inventing state categories from the data.
