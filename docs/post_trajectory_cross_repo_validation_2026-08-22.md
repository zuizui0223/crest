## 6. Predictive-state boundary

The trajectory reframe does not claim that state-as-future-prediction is new.

- computational mechanics already groups histories by predictive future distributions;
- Predictive State Representations represent controlled-system state through multi-step action-conditional predictions / future tests;
- POMDP and state-abstraction methods can encode rich hidden history, mechanism, action and observation structure.

CREST's safe distinction is typed ecology-specific architecture and cross-gate diagnosis, not non-embeddability or greater expressive power.
