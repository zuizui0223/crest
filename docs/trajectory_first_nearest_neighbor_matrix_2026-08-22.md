## Decision rule

For every CREST headline, separate four things:

1. what the nearest prior literature already establishes;
2. what CREST adds or reorganizes;
3. wording that is safe;
4. wording that is blocked unless a stronger theorem is proved.

## Current novelty-preserving center

The safe headline is not temporal thickness by itself. It is:

> **CREST couples future/composition, inherited semantics/history, retained mechanism responses, carrier feasibility, and evidence licensing into one ecological state-equivalence problem, while separating the state required by the scientific contract from the state identified by evidence and the target that can still be reported.**

The strongest theorem-grounded support for that headline is the finite carrier → joint-state → evidence chain plus the strict action-expansion and repair/evidence noncommutation witnesses.
