# CREST manuscript scope change — 2026-08-24

The canonical CREST manuscript no longer uses empirical validation, prospective Izu/Campanula testing, or restoration/conservation case studies as part of its argumentative spine.

The paper closes as:

1. **Philosophy** — temporally extended ecological worlds, contract-relative state, quotient-level law.
2. **Finite mathematics** — carrier feasibility, least-information state, evidence licensing, capability–resolution divergence.
3. **Ecological projection** — implications for ecological state variables, functional equivalence, history/mechanism relevance, monitoring, and representational stability.

Empirical programmes remain independent repository assets and future research directions. They do not enter the manuscript as validation sections or Discussion arcs.
