# CREST publication sequence — 2026-08-18

## Decision

Do **not** wait until four companion short reports have all been published before submitting CREST. Do **not** fragment the companion programs into thin notes merely to create citations.

Use the following sequence:

```text
CCOC flagship full paper / citable preprint
  -> CREST philosophy-of-ecology paper
  -> MLTR, MRM, and CED as independent focused full papers
  -> later formal CREST synthesis paper only after a genuine new coupling/application
```

This is a `1 + 1 + 3` strategy, not `4 + 1`.

## Why CCOC goes first

CCOC owns the clearest standalone obstruction:

> individual closed-future compressibility need not imply comparably small exact compression under a jointly opened future grammar.

Its cross-grammar lower bound and bounded-local sharpness can stand without CREST. A public preprint or submitted manuscript gives the CREST article one concrete formal anchor without forcing the metatheory to wait for every companion paper.

CCOC should be a full theorem article rather than a short communication unless the eventual journal imposes a concise format. The lower bound, sharpness family, prior-art firewall, and ecological interpretation need room to remain auditable.

## Why CREST should not wait for all four companions

CREST's main contribution is not a retrospective summary of four published papers. It is the claim that ecological state equivalence represents distinct commitments concerning future sufficiency, inherited meaning, retained mechanism alternatives, and evidential licensing, together with cross-contract synthesis results and failure boundaries.

Waiting for all four companion papers would:

1. delay the concept until it appears derivative rather than program-forming;
2. encourage salami-sliced short reports;
3. make the four axes look like post-hoc branding;
4. create avoidable circular dependencies between manuscripts; and
5. expose the program to terminology drift before the upper claim is fixed publicly.

A citable CCOC anchor plus versioned public theorem/proof repositories is enough to submit the CREST philosophy article while the other companions remain in preparation or review, provided every theorem-level claim is traceable and unpublished status is disclosed accurately.

## Journal lanes

### Current CREST philosophy manuscript

Primary lane: **Biology & Philosophy**.

The manuscript should remain a philosophy-of-ecology article with formal anchors, not become a catalogue of J1–J7 proofs. Its central argument is representational and epistemological:

> treating ecological configurations as the same state is a contract-relative scientific commitment about which differences may be ignored.

Theorems function as disciplined counterexamples, boundaries, and witnesses. Full proofs remain in the repository or supplement.

Stretch lane: **Philosophy of Science**, only after rewriting the paper so the ecology case supports a broader argument about state representation across sciences. The current ecology-specific framing is better aligned with Biology & Philosophy.

### Companion theorem papers

- **CCOC:** first formal flagship; mathematical/theoretical ecology lane.
- **MLTR:** full source-relative transport/repair paper after monitoring material is kept outside its headline claim.
- **MRM:** full mechanism-robust reporting paper; candidate-safe quotient and ambiguity frontier are the center.
- **CED:** full evidence/reportability and target-safe monitoring paper; failure architecture and risk-limited reporting are the center.

The companions should not be called short reports by default. Format follows the amount of proof and ecological interpretation required, not a desire to multiply outputs.

## Submission gates

CREST may be submitted after all of the following hold:

1. CCOC has a stable citable preprint or is under journal review.
2. The CREST repository independently reproduces J1–J7 and O1.
3. Every FORMAL manuscript claim links to an analytic proof and executable witness.
4. Companion ownership and prior-art firewalls are explicit.
5. The text does not imply that all four companion papers are already published.
6. Final human source, proof, interpretation, and wording review is complete.
7. Author metadata, funding, competing interests, and AI-use disclosure are finalized.

## Later mathematical CREST paper

A second CREST paper should not merely restate the J-series. It requires at least one genuinely new cross-contract result, such as:

- a necessary-and-sufficient commutation boundary;
- a sharp joint impossibility;
- a nontrivial coupled minimum beyond generic fixed-point or repair machinery;
- or an ecological application demonstrating a failure that no companion axis can diagnose alone.

Until then, the philosophy paper is the CREST flagship, and theorem expansion remains frozen.
