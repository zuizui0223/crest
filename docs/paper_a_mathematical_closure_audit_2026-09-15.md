# Paper A mathematical closure audit — 2026-09-15

## Verdict

The current finite Paper A spine is definitionally acyclic and internally closed **conditional on declared pre-state semantics**. It is not a theorem that arbitrary canonical MLTR, MRM, and CCOC objects admit one simultaneous common realization. That stronger representation/bridge statement remains outside the Paper A claim set.

## 1. Dependency DAG

The permitted dependency order is

\[
(\Omega,O_t;\ q_r,\{R_e\},p;\ Q,A,\{G_a^\theta\};\ \mathcal M,\mathcal L)
\to
(H_t,\Theta_t,F_t)
\to
\text{declared prerequisite/access structure}
\to
Q_*.
\]

There is no permitted edge from the final quotient `Q_*` back into `q_r`, a replacement relation/path, a primitive candidate law, the controlled law, or the future grammar. Thus the final state cannot be used to manufacture the distinctions that justify that state.

## 2. What is mathematically closed

For a finite carrier and pre-state signatures `g_i` declared independently of the final state,

\[
\omega\sim_*\omega'
\iff
O_t(\omega)=O_t(\omega')
\ \text{and}\
g_i(\omega)=g_i(\omega')\ \forall i
\]

defines a unique coarsest admissible quotient, up to relabeling. This is the finite state-existence/minimality result used by Paper A.

The prospective semantic-access layer is then conditional on declared objects:

- retained history modes `H_min`;
- retained response types `Theta`;
- a declared future query `f`;
- its prerequisite set `R_f`;
- its semantic access relation `A_f subset H_min x Theta`;
- decoder depth `m_f` and occupancy measure.

Given those objects, the Shannon support theorem and Hartley support-count theorem are exact finite consequences. They do not require the final quotient to define their own inputs.

## 3. What is not closed, and is not required for Paper A

Paper A does **not** prove:

1. a canonical decomposition of every ecological world into independent past × mechanism × future coordinates;
2. a canonical common lift for arbitrary MLTR, MRM, and CCOC models;
3. that the semantic access relation `A_f` is uniquely generated by those three companion repositories without an additional synchronization/contract;
4. that every legitimate ecological state obligation belongs to exactly one of the three temporal roles;
5. a self-originating root semantic map for MLTR;
6. continuous-time, stochastic, approximate, or infinite-state generality.

These are representation/realizability extensions, not hidden premises of the finite sparse-access results.

## 4. The key realizability firewall

The strict companion-realizability no-go remains binding. In particular:

- immutable raw history prevents the old zero-debt future audit from being activated merely by retaining history;
- under one fixed MRM intervention grammar, an already candidate-safe visible partition cannot conceal multiple exact response types that later appear only after an unrelated fixed partition is added;
- positive interaction cannot be obtained merely by intersecting fixed precomputed companion partitions on a one-class cut.

Therefore old abstract closure cascades must not be reinterpreted as literal simultaneous MLTR × MRM × CCOC executions.

The v0.7 semantic-access construction avoids that invalid inference by taking derived history modes and response types as pre-state semantic interfaces and declaring where a future query is licensed on their semantic product. Its exact quantitative results are conditional on that declared access contract.

## 5. Circularity stress test

A proposed Paper A definition fails the firewall if any of the following occurs:

- `H_t` is defined using `Q_*`;
- `Theta_t` is defined using `Q_*`;
- `F_t` or the legal grammar is chosen by inspecting `Q_*`;
- `A_f` is defined as “the cells that make the desired dividend/theorem true” rather than as an independently declared semantic/legal relation;
- a prerequisite set is inferred from the same Möbius support that is then presented as evidence for that prerequisite set.

The last item is especially important after the Shannon revision. Paper A may prove

\[
R_f\ \text{declared independently}
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{supp}(d_1)=R_f\cup\{F\}
\]

for the stated query family. It must not define `R_f` from `supp(d_1)` and then claim support fidelity as an independent discovery.

## 6. Publication claim boundary

Safe headline:

> Given independently specified finite retrospective, latent-response, and prospective semantic obligations at a temporal cut, there is a unique coarsest state preserving them. For prospective queries with independently declared prerequisite and semantic-access structure, Shannon interaction support tracks prerequisite topology while the Hartley endpoint quantifies support-space contraction under sparse access.

Unsafe stronger headline:

> Every ecological system canonically decomposes into MLTR history, MRM mechanism, and CCOC future coordinates, and CREST proves that this decomposition is uniquely realized.

The second statement is not proved.
