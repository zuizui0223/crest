# CREST temporal state interaction theorem

## Decision

CREST should treat the visible present as the **baseline descriptive surface**, not
as one obstruction axis among several. A temporally extended ecological world is
written schematically as

\[
\omega=(H_{<t},X_t,\Theta_t,F_{>t}),
\]

where

- \(H_{<t}\) is relevant retrospective history;
- \(X_t\) is the visible present snapshot;
- \(\Theta_t\) is latent contemporaneous response structure not resolved by that
  snapshot; and
- \(F_{>t}\) is the declared prospective response structure.

The present-snapshot partition \(B_X\) is the baseline. CREST then asks which
retrospective, latent-present, and prospective distinctions must be retained for
the declared scientific responsibility.

This is a mathematical re-expression of the existing trajectory-first state
concept, not a claim that these three dimensions exhaust every legitimate source
of ecological state distinction.

## 1. Temporal-position closures

On one finite common carrier \(U\), let

\[
C_H,\qquad C_\Theta,\qquad C_F
\]

be refinement closures acting above the visible-present baseline \(B_X\).
Respectively they encode required distinctions generated by history, latent
present response structure, and prospective response obligations.

For a coalition \(S\subseteq\{H,\Theta,F\}\), let \(J_S\) be its least common
fixed point above \(B_X\), and define

\[
v(S)=\log_2|J_S|-\log_2|B_X|,
\qquad v(\varnothing)=0.
\]

The three-axis joint debt is

\[
D_{H\Theta F}=v(H\Theta F),
\]

and the standalone debts are \(D_H=v(H)\), \(D_\Theta=v(\Theta)\), and
\(D_F=v(F)\).

The exact Möbius interaction decomposition is

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_{H\Theta F}
={}&D_H+D_\Theta+D_F\\
&+m(H,\Theta)+m(H,F)+m(\Theta,F)\\
&+m(H,\Theta,F),
\end{aligned}
\]

with pairwise terms such as

\[
m(H,F)=v(HF)-v(H)-v(F),
\]

and the genuine three-way term

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
m(H,\Theta,F)
={}&v(H\Theta F)-v(H\Theta)-v(HF)-v(\Theta F)\\
&+v(H)+v(\Theta)+v(F).
\end{aligned}
}
\]

This is cooperative-game/Möbius accounting applied to the CREST coalition value
function. The generic algebra is standard; the substantive question is whether
these temporal-position responsibilities can generate nonzero or unbounded terms
on one ecological state construction.

## 2. Theorem A — past–future interaction can be unbounded

For every integer \(n\ge2\), there is a finite CREST contract in which all latent
worlds share one visible present snapshot and

\[
D_H=1,\qquad D_F=0,
\]

but

\[
D_{HF}=\log_2 n.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{m(H,F)=\log_2 n-1,}
\]

which is unbounded.

### Construction

Take an \(n\)-world indiscrete visible-present baseline. The history closure marks
one world and therefore creates a two-class partition. The future closure is a
directed cycle. On the untouched baseline the cycle has zero debt because all
successors remain in the same block. Once the historical mark is retained,
successor stability propagates that mark around the cycle until every world is
distinct.

### Interpretation

A history difference can be irrelevant to the visible present and still become
arbitrarily expensive to forget once a future-response responsibility is imposed.
Thus “how much of the past the present state must remember” cannot in general be
computed independently of what future the state is expected to support.

This is **interaction/coupling**, not statistical confounding. No probabilistic
claim about common causes or regression bias is intended.

## 3. Theorem B — genuine three-way temporal interaction is unbounded

For every integer \(m\ge2\), there exists a finite CREST contract with a single
visible-present baseline class and three temporal-position closures satisfying

\[
D_H=1,
\qquad
D_\Theta=0,
\qquad
D_F=0,
\]

while the full joint state has exactly

\[
|J_{H\Theta F}|=2^m
\]

classes, hence

\[
\boxed{D_{H\Theta F}=m\text{ bits}.}
\]

The nontrivial coalition debts are

\[
v(H\Theta)=\log_2 3,
\qquad
v(HF)=1,
\]

with \(v(\Theta F)=0\). Therefore the only nonzero higher-order dividends are

\[
\boxed{m(H,\Theta)=\log_2(3/2)}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{m(H,\Theta,F)=m-\log_2 3.}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{m(H,\Theta,F)\to\infty}
\]

as \(m\to\infty\), and

\[
\boxed{
\frac{m(H,\Theta,F)}{D_{H\Theta F}}
=1-\frac{\log_2 3}{m}
\longrightarrow1.
}
\]

Thus the genuine three-way temporal interaction can asymptotically dominate the
entire required state information.

### Construction

Use \(2^m+1\) latent worlds with one visible-present baseline class.

1. `HISTORY` marks one world, producing two classes.
2. `LATENT_PRESENT` has one response edge from a second world into the history
   seed. It is inert on the baseline, but after the history split it creates a
   third class.
3. `FUTURE` contains a chain of \(2^m-3\) worlds. It is inert before the latent
   split. Once that split exists, successor consistency propagates it down the
   chain.
4. Two residual worlds remain merged, so the final joint partition has exactly
   \(2^m\) classes.

Coalitions not containing the required upstream activation remain dormant. In
particular, `HISTORY + FUTURE` remains at two classes and
`LATENT_PRESENT + FUTURE` remains at one.

### Proof of the debt formulas

The coalition block counts are exactly

| coalition | blocks |
|---|---:|
| none | 1 |
| H | 2 |
| Theta | 1 |
| F | 1 |
| H + Theta | 3 |
| H + F | 2 |
| Theta + F | 1 |
| H + Theta + F | \(2^m\) |

Taking \(\log_2\) relative to the one-block baseline gives the stated coalition
values. Möbius inversion then yields

\[
m(H,\Theta)=\log_2 3-1=\log_2(3/2),
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
m(H,\Theta,F)
&=m-\log_2 3-1-0+1+0+0\\
&=m-\log_2 3.
\end{aligned}
\]

No asymptotic approximation is used.

## 4. Numerical anchor at m = 10

For \(m=10\), all worlds still share the same visible present baseline, but the
joint adequate state has

\[
2^{10}=1024
\]

classes and exactly **10 bits** of debt.

The decomposition is

| component | bits | share of joint debt |
|---|---:|---:|
| direct history | 1.0000000000 | 10.00% |
| history x latent-present | 0.5849625007 | 5.85% |
| history x future | 0 | 0% |
| latent-present x future | 0 | 0% |
| **history x latent-present x future** | **8.4150374993** | **84.15%** |
| **joint** | **10.0000000000** | **100%** |

Total interaction debt is

\[
\Delta=10-1=9\text{ bits},
\]

so **90% of the joint debt is interaction-generated**. Of those nine interaction
bits, **93.50%** belong to the genuine three-way dividend.

This is a stronger endpoint than saying only that joint debt is non-additive. In
this family, the state information is eventually dominated by distinctions that
belong to no temporal-position axis in isolation.

## 5. What is now mathematically recovered

The temporal-state reading has four levels.

1. **State concept — recovered.** The state is a coarsest scientifically adequate
   compression of temporally extended worlds, with the visible present used as a
   possible baseline description.
2. **Temporal-position axes — recovered relative to a declared finite contract.**
   Retrospective history, latent contemporaneous structure, and prospective
   response can be represented by distinct refinement closures on the same
   present-snapshot carrier.
3. **Pairwise temporal interaction — recovered.** Past-by-future interaction can
   be unbounded even when the future axis has zero standalone debt.
4. **Higher-order temporal interaction — recovered.** Genuine
   history-by-latent-present-by-future interaction can be unbounded and can
   asymptotically account for all of the joint state debt.

## 6. What is not recovered

The following stronger claims remain unsupported and should not be stated.

- that history, latent present, and future exhaust every legitimate ecological
  state responsibility;
- that every ecological system admits a unique natural decomposition into these
  coordinates;
- that the latent mechanism coordinate is statistically identifiable from the
  visible present;
- a continuous-time, infinite-state, stochastic, or approximate analogue of the
  finite theorem;
- a theorem that the direction of activation must follow chronological order;
- any use of “confounding” in the statistical-identification sense.

The proved result is conditional and finite: **once a common present-snapshot
carrier and temporal-position responsibility closures are declared, their
interaction can dominate the least-information adequate state.**
