### Comparing contract-relative obstruction profiles

The joint-debt analysis can also be used comparatively. For a fixed named responsibility set, the Möbius/Harsanyi dividends of the coalition debt function provide a complete finite coordinate representation of the obstruction accounting profile. If `m_A(S)` and `m_B(S)` are the dividends under two declared contracts, CREST defines the raw profile distance

\[
d_1(A,B)=\sum_{\varnothing\ne S}|m_A(S)-m_B(S)|
\]

and its Euclidean analogue `d_2`. Singleton coordinates quantify direct responsibility differences; higher-order coordinates quantify differences in interaction anatomy. Thus the L1 distance decomposes exactly into direct and interaction components.

In the canonical CCOC/MLTR/MRM activation example, all singleton coordinates remain unchanged. The only changes are the pairwise CCOC×MLTR term, `+0.4150374993 bit`, and the three-way CCOC×MLTR×MRM term, `+0.3219280949 bit`. The resulting raw L1 distance is therefore `0.7369655942 bit`, entirely interaction-generated. On the same six-world, two-state baseline, normalizing by the maximum available refinement capacity `log2(3)` gives a normalized L1 profile distance of `0.4649735207`.

This comparison should not be interpreted as a biological distance between ecosystems. It is a distance between complete finite obstruction-accounting profiles. Cross-system use requires commensurable responsibility meanings, carrier construction, and baseline semantics. The L1/L2 and Möbius mathematics are standard; the CREST role is the contract-relative ecological interpretation of the resulting profile geometry.
