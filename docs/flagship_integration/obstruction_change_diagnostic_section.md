# Flagship integration — state-debt change can be interaction-only

> **Role:** follow the static joint-debt result with a before/after diagnostic showing that representational burden can change even when every responsibility-wise standalone score is unchanged.

## From static debt to representational change

The joint-debt quantity describes one declared scientific contract. Conservation and management problems, however, are not static: interventions become feasible, replacement histories are reinterpreted, and mechanism responsibilities change. CREST therefore compares obstruction spectra across two declared contracts on the same named responsibility set.

Write the before and after joint debts as `D_joint^-` and `D_joint^+`, and define the change

\[
\delta D_{\rm joint}=D_{\rm joint}^+-D_{\rm joint}^-.
\]

For the standalone debts and non-additive excess,

\[
\boxed{
\delta D_{\rm joint}
=
\sum_i\delta D_i+
\delta\Delta.
}
\]

This identity separates two kinds of representational change. A **direct** change occurs when one or more responsibilities demand a different amount of state resolution in isolation. An **interaction** change occurs when the non-additive coupling among responsibilities changes. The two can occur separately or together.

The important boundary is the interaction-only case:

\[
\boxed{
\delta D_i=0\ \forall i,
\quad
\delta D_{\rm joint}\ne0
\Longrightarrow
\delta D_{\rm joint}=\delta\Delta.
}
\]

Thus a system can become harder or easier to represent jointly even though every responsibility appears unchanged when inspected alone.

## Numeric three-obstruction witness

In the six-world CCOC/MLTR/MRM comparison, the before contract requires three states and the after contract five. Joint debt therefore changes from

\[
0.5849625007
\quad\text{to}\quad
1.3219280949\ \text{bits},
\]

an increase of

\[
\boxed{+0.7369655942\ \text{bit}.}
\]

Yet

\[
\delta D_{\rm CCOC}
=
\delta D_{\rm MLTR}
=
\delta D_{\rm MRM}
=0.
\]

The whole increase is interaction-generated. Möbius decomposition identifies its order:

\[
+0.7369655942
=
\underbrace{+0.4150374993}_{\text{CCOC x MLTR}}
+
\underbrace{+0.3219280949}_{\text{CCOC x MLTR x MRM}}
\quad\text{bits}.
\]

The compact diagnosis is therefore

```text
source class:      interaction-only
burden direction:  increase
active orders:     2, 3
dominant order:    2
```

The reverse change produces an interaction-only decrease with the same active orders and opposite signs.

## Ecological interpretation

This strengthens the monitoring implication of the static non-additivity theorem. A manager can re-audit future sufficiency, inherited semantics, and mechanism robustness separately and conclude that none has become more demanding. That conclusion does not imply that the old monitoring resolution remains adequate. The coupling among responsibilities may have changed even when the marginal burden of each responsibility has not.

Accordingly, representational stability should be assessed by comparing the **joint obstruction spectrum**, not only by tracking responsibility-wise scores. An interaction-only increase means that new monitoring demand is generated by how scientific responsibilities compose, not by a larger standalone demand from any one component theory.

## Claim boundary

The before/after arithmetic and Möbius decomposition are established mathematical substrate. The CREST contribution is the ecological-state interpretation: contract changes can create or remove representational burden entirely through cross-responsibility coupling, and the finite obstruction spectrum makes that change measurable in bits.
