# CREST AmNat v0.3 readiness audit — 2026-09-08

## Decision

The temporal-cut theorem is mathematically ready enough to stop adding theorem families. The remaining pre-submission work is manuscript positioning and journal compliance.

## Blocking manuscript issues found

1. **Abstract length.** The current v0.3 abstract is about 282 words; The American Naturalist Major Article limit is 200 words.
2. **Keywords.** The current manuscript lists eight keywords; the journal asks for one to six.
3. **Title length.** The current two-line title is much longer than the journal's preferred concise title range. A 9-word replacement is: `Ecological State at a Temporal Cut: Interaction Across Time`.
4. **Literature positioning.** v0.3 currently has no Literature Cited section. This makes the novelty firewall unsupported in the manuscript even though the repository's internal novelty audit is careful.
5. **Methods/Results signaling.** The mathematical construction is present, but section labels do not make the journal-preferred Methods-before-Results organization obvious.

## Required prior-art anchors

The manuscript should explicitly distinguish CREST from at least four neighboring literatures.

- ecological memory / antecedent effects: Ogle et al. 2015;
- ecological hysteresis and regime/state shifts: Scheffer et al. 2001; transient dynamics: Hastings et al. 2018;
- predictive/casual state representations: Shalizi and Crutchfield 2001; Littman, Sutton, and Singh 2001;
- generic state abstraction / bisimulation in decision systems: Givan, Dean, and Greig 2003; Li, Walsh, and Littman 2006.

The intended boundary is:

> Those literatures establish that past conditions, future predictions, latent state, and task-preserving abstraction can matter. CREST does not claim those facts. CREST fixes one observational temporal cut and proves that retrospective, latent-contemporaneous, and prospective responsibilities acting on the same cut need not be separable; their interaction can be unbounded and can asymptotically dominate the required state information.

## Submission-facing target

No empirical material should be added merely to satisfy the journal. The current paper is a theory Major Article. The next manuscript should retain the finite/exact scope firewall and make the conceptual payoff legible before the closure machinery.
